## Why multi-agent

Human teams work because each person has a role and a definition of done. Same idea here: the planner breaks the requirement into tasks, the coder implements, the reviewer critiques and the tester validates — and the loop repeats until it passes.

## Why a monorepo

Types, contracts and utilities shared by web, api and worker live in internal packages. A contract change breaks the build immediately, not in production.
